  \huge\textbf{Declaration on the Use of Generative AI}

\vspace{1em}

{\linespread{1.05}\selectfont

In accordance with the regulation of the Faculty of Computer Science on the use of generative artificial intelligence (AI) in theses, I disclose the following use of AI systems in the preparation of this thesis.

\vspace{0.6em}
\noindent I used Claude (Anthropic), models Opus~5 and Opus~5.5, from July to November 2026, through the Claude Code development environment and the Claude.ai chat interface with its connector to Overleaf. It served as a programming and writing assistant, so that the experiments could be implemented and documented faster. The table gives the level of use for each kind of work: \emph{generated} (the AI produced a first version, which I checked and revised), \emph{drafted} (the AI wrote text from my results and instructions, which I revised and modified), \emph{assisted} (the AI found, proposed or checked information) and \emph{edited} (the AI changed the wording of existing text).

\vspace{1em}

  Implementation & generated & All code for feature extraction, experiments, statistics and figures, written and run by the AI from my specifications. I checked the outputs against data counts and published results. \\
  Experiment design and analysis & assisted & The AI proposed analyses and controls, from which I selected. It computed the results and drafted interpretations, which I checked against the numbers. \\
  Figures and tables & generated & Drawn by scripts and TikZ code that the AI wrote, from the saved results. \\
  Literature & assisted & The AI found sources and checked every bibliographic entry. I selected the sources cited. \\
  Thesis text & drafted & First drafts of all chapters, Appendix~A and the abstract, written from my results and instructions, which I revised and modified. \\
