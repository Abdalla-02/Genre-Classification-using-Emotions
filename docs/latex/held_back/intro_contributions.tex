The thesis makes three contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A pipeline that places eight rated emotions between film audio and genre is compared with six audio representations used directly, under the same evaluation protocol.
  \item The emotions that characterise each film genre in the listener ratings are described. They give a reference against which the emotion step can be read.
  \item The results suggest that the lead of the emotion step appears at the default decision threshold, on the small dataset and when a model trained on one dataset is applied to the Blockbuster dataset, and that much of it disappears when the threshold is tuned per genre.
\end{enumerate}
