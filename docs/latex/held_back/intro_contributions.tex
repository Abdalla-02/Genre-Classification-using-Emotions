The thesis makes three contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item A pipeline that places eight rated emotions between film audio and genre is compared with six audio representations used directly, under the same evaluation protocol.
  \item The emotions that characterise each film genre in the listener ratings are described, which makes the predictions of the emotion step interpretable.
  \item The results suggest that the emotion step helps most when training data are scarce or when a model trained on one dataset is applied to the Blockbuster dataset, and they show where this advantage depends on the decision threshold.
\end{enumerate}
