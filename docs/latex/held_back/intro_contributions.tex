The thesis makes three contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item I place eight rated emotions between film audio and genre and compare this pipeline with six audio representations used directly, under the same evaluation protocol.
  \item I describe which emotions characterise each film genre in the listener ratings, which makes the predictions of the emotion step interpretable.
  \item The results suggest that the emotion step helps most when training data are scarce or when a model trained on one dataset is applied to the Blockbuster dataset, and they show where this advantage depends on the decision threshold.
\end{enumerate}
