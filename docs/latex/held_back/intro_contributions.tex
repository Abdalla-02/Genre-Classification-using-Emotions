The thesis makes three contributions:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item I place eight rated emotions between film audio and genre and compare this pipeline with six audio representations used directly, under the same evaluation protocol.
  \item I describe the emotional signatures of film genres and test whether they recur on a second, independent set of films.
  \item The results suggest that the emotion step helps most when training data are scarce or the dataset changes, and they show where this advantage depends on the decision threshold.
\end{enumerate}
